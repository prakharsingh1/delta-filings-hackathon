Where the data comes from

SEBI
Circulars, orders, recovery proceedings and press releases, from SEBI's own feed. Orders naming a counterparty become risk signals.
RBI
Notifications and press releases, tagged by department and topic.
IBBI
Public announcements of insolvency and liquidation. This works for unlisted companies too.
NSE
Corporate announcements, results and share prices for listed companies you watch or lend to.
You
Entity profiles, events, meetings, registers, SDD entries and anything your risk team logs by hand.

What it does not do.

  • It does not file on the MCA, GST or income-tax portals. It tells you what is due and what the delay costs, and drafts the letters and meeting papers.
  • MCA does not publish a feed we can read, so MCA notifications are not in the news yet.
  • Unlisted MCA data such as charges and struck-off status needs a licensed source, and is not included.
  • India first. Every rule carries a jurisdiction code, so another country can be added as data rather than as a rewrite.
