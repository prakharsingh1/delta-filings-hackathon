Methodology

Sources. Exchange filings of listed counterparties (NSE announcements, including the attached letter where available); SEBI orders and recovery proceedings from the SEBI feed and SEBI's enforcement records; IBBI public announcements of insolvency and liquidation, matched by name; one-day share-price falls of 5% or more; and signals logged by the risk team. Board composition comes from quarterly corporate governance filings.

Scoring. Each signal adds {{ weights.critical }} points if critical, {{ weights.high }} if high, {{ weights.medium }} if medium and {{ weights.low }} if low. Points fade with the age of the event: full weight for 30 days, 70% to 90 days, 45% to 180 days, 25% to a year, then nothing. Resolved signals count at half weight and dismissed ones not at all. Repeats of the same kind of signal count at half, then a quarter, and filings about the same event within a week are merged. The score is capped at 100.

Bands. Severe 60 and above, High 35 to 59, Moderate 15 to 34, Low under 15. An open critical signal (insolvency admission, payment default, fraud) from the last 90 days puts a name in Severe on its own. Exposure at risk is the recorded exposure to names in the High and Severe bands.

Limitations. Unlisted companies are covered only through IBBI announcements, SEBI orders and the team's own entries. MCA data such as charges, filing defaults and strike-off status is not yet covered. Name matches marked "verify" need a human check. Exposures are as entered by the team.
