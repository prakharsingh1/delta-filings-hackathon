How the chain works

Each entry stores a SHA-256 fingerprint of its own contents combined with the fingerprint of the entry before it. Changing, deleting or reordering any past entry breaks every fingerprint after it. UPSI records, trading-window closures, pre-clearance decisions and the designated persons list go into a second chain of the same kind. A separate record of how long both chains are shows when the newest entries are deleted. Re-verify checks all of it, and every UPSI record against what was logged for it.

Someone with direct access to the database could still rebuild every record consistently. Record the head hash in the audit committee minutes each quarter: a recorded head that is missing from the CSV's Hash column shows that.
