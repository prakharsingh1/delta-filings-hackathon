Why per company

A seat count does not describe the work.

A practice with three people may look after eighty companies. A listed company's compliance officer may be the only user. Charging by company keeps the price tied to the number of calendars you rely on, and lets everyone who needs to see the calendar see it.

GST is added to all prices. A two-year prepayment discount and a cap on price rises are planned.
