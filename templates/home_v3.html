In practice

From the circular to the filing.

One listed company, one quarter. Every step below happens inside Delta Filings. Filing on the exchange and the MCA portal is still yours to do.

  1. 01Regulatory news

    Any day

    A SEBI circular lands

    It is tagged LODR and shows under For you, because you look after a listed company.

  2. 02Insider trading

    Quarter end

    The trading window closes

    Pre-clearance requests from designated persons are refused until it reopens.

  3. 03Calendar

    Meeting scheduled

    Reg 29 goes on the calendar

    The prior intimation to the exchange is dated two days before the board meeting.

  4. 04Meeting kit

    7 days before

    Notice and agenda go out

    Pick items from the agenda library. The kit writes the notice, agenda, draft resolutions and a minutes outline as one .docx.

  5. 05SDD

    Results shared

    Every recipient of UPSI is logged

    Entries are time-stamped in IST and hash-chained. There is no edit button. A mistake gets a correction entry that points at the original.

  6. 06Draft

    Meeting day

    The outcome letter is drafted

    Reg 30 gives you 30 minutes. The draft follows your company's past outcome letters. You review it, export it and file it on NSE.

  7. 07Calendar

    Marked held

    The follow-ups are dated

    Minutes signing and MGT-14, where the resolution needs it, fall due within 30 days. If a director was appointed, DIR-12 joins them.

Where the data comes from

SEBI
Circulars, orders, recovery proceedings and press releases, from SEBI's own feed. Orders naming a counterparty become risk signals.
RBI
Notifications and press releases, tagged by department and topic.
IBBI
Public announcements of insolvency and liquidation. This works for unlisted companies too.
NSE
Corporate announcements, results and share prices for listed companies you watch or lend to.
You
Entity profiles, events, meetings, registers, SDD entries and anything your risk team logs by hand.

What it does not do

  • It does not file on the MCA, GST or income-tax portals. It tells you what is due and what the delay costs, and drafts the letters and meeting papers.
  • MCA does not publish a feed we can read, so MCA notifications are not in the news yet.
  • Unlisted MCA data such as charges and struck-off status needs a licensed source, and is not included.

India first. Every rule carries a jurisdiction code, so another country can be added as data rather than as a rewrite.
