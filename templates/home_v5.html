In practice · one quarter

From the circular to the filing.

One listed company, one quarter. Every step below happens inside Delta Filings. Filing on the exchange and the MCA portal is still yours to do.

Activity · Sabari Cables Ltd7 entries

  1. It is tagged LODR and shows under For you, because you look after a listed company.

  2. Pre-clearance requests from designated persons are refused until it reopens.

  3. The prior intimation to the exchange is dated two days before the board meeting.

  4. Pick items from the agenda library. The kit writes the notice, agenda, draft resolutions and a minutes outline as one .docx.

  5. Entries are time-stamped in IST and hash-chained. There is no edit button. A mistake gets a correction entry that points at the original.

  6. Reg 30 gives you 30 minutes. The draft follows your company's past outcome letters. You review it, export it and file it on NSE.

  7. Minutes signing and MGT-14, where the resolution needs it, fall due within 30 days. If a director was appointed, DIR-12 joins them.

Filing on the exchange and the MCA portal stays with you.
