About

Compliance software that shows its working.

Indian companies answer to the MCA, SEBI, the exchanges, the tax departments, RBI and the labour authorities, each with its own portal, its own dates and its own penalties. Delta Filings puts those obligations in one place, with the source and the arithmetic visible.

How we build

Four rules we hold ourselves to.

Every rule cites its source.

A due date with no law behind it is a guess. Each rule names the section, links to where it comes from and carries the date it was last reviewed.

Say what is estimated.

Late fees depend on facts we may not have. We label them indicative and show how the figure was reached. Where we cannot work it out, we say so rather than invent a number.

Check the corrections twice.

The rulebook was fact-checked against official sources, and every correction was checked again by a second, independent pass before it went in.

Say what is not built.

The security, FAQ and product pages list the gaps next to the features. A buyer should not find them out later.

Who it is for

The people who answer for compliance.

Company secretaries, practising CS firms, compliance officers of listed companies, and risk officers who watch counterparties. See each role.

What it is not

  • It does not file on the MCA portal. MCA has no public API.
  • It does not replace your accounting or payroll software.
  • It is not legal advice. It tells you what the rules say and what lateness costs; you decide what to do.
