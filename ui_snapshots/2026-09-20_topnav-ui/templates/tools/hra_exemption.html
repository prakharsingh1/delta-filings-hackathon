The three statutory limits
Note: Under Section 10(13A) and Rule 2A, exempt HRA is the least of: (a) actual HRA received, (b) rent paid minus 10% of salary, and (c) 50% of salary (metro) or 40% (non-metro), where salary = basic + DA. HRA exemption is available only under the Old Tax Regime — it is not allowed under the New Regime. Figures are annual. Verify with your tax advisor.
