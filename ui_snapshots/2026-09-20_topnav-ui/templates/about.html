About Delta Filings

One platform for every
corporate governance signal.

Delta Filings unifies SEBI filings, compliance deadlines, live stock prices, board-governance data and AI-powered document intelligence into a single, analyst-grade workspace for Indian listed companies.

What it does

Seven capabilities, one workspace

Everything you'd otherwise stitch together from NSE, BSE, MCA, Bloomberg, and a pile of PDFs.

Real-time Filing Tracker

Core

Fetch, store and browse every NSE corporate announcement for your watchlist — live.

✓Automatic filing ingestion per company
✓Full-text PDF extraction & on-demand re-indexing
✓Smart tag detection (board meetings, results, rating, key-personnel, etc.)
✓Search, pagination & tag filters inside each company
✓"New since last visit" highlighting via snapshots

AI Summaries

AI

Understand any filing in seconds — no more scrolling 80-page PDFs.

✓One-click AI summary of any filing
✓Pick between Fast and Deep AI models
✓Graceful fallback to extractive summaries when AI is unavailable
✓Cached per filing to stay fast & cheap
✓Regenerate on demand for a fresh take

Compliance

SEBI

Track every SEBI LODR & PIT obligation across your watchlist, automatically.

✓12+ SEBI mandates (Reg 13, 27, 29, 30, 31, 33, 39, 44, 74 + PIT Reg 7)
✓Buckets: Upcoming, Overdue, Completed, Future — reconciled & verified
✓Per-company compliance score (0–100) with health tags
✓Automatic filing → obligation linkage by keyword & date window
✓Direct links to official SEBI source documents on sebi.gov.in

Live Stock Prices & Alerts

Live

Prices, pre-open data and sector context — explained by recent filings.

✓Live NSE quotes for every watchlist stock
✓3% price-move alerts cross-referenced with recent filings
✓Pre-open session data with early-move detection
✓NIFTY FMCG / sector view with correlated movers
✓"Why did it move?" narrative using filings & peers

Corporate Governance Reports

Board

Structured board & committee data from NSE governance filings.

✓Quarterly governance report parsing (XBRL + text)
✓Board composition, tenure & independent-director data
✓Compare any two reports & see exactly what changed
✓Audit & nomination committee snapshots

Directors

Track a director across every company you watch — in one query.

✓Search any Director Identification Number across the full watchlist
✓Finds matches in both filing text and governance XBRL data
✓Year filter for time-windowed investigations
✓Board-role and tenure surfaced from governance reports
